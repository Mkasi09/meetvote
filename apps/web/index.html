Create a candidate election

Reusable election templates

Save common vote setups and reuse them for another meeting. Candidate names, profiles, and optional photos are saved with candidate templates.

For an existing draft, you can edit its title and schedule. Its ballot choices are fixed; use a template to create the same kind of vote at a future meeting with different choices.

Candidate profiles

Every candidate needs a full name, position, short biography, and experience or qualifications. Add a photo when available; otherwise, the default portrait is used. You can preview and reorder profiles before creating the election.

For an existing draft, candidate names and ballot choices are fixed. Update profile details, photographs, or order only.

Eligible residents can submit one confidential ballot. While a meeting is live, omitted times default to now and two hours from now.
